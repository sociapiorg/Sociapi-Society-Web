# Sociapi Society backlink outreach

Use these as starting drafts. Personalize every note, confirm the recipient and facts, and make the request only where a link would help that site's audience. A link is optional; don't offer payment or request keyword-rich anchor text.

## University department or student affairs team

**Subject:** Student technology community information for your students

Hello [Name],

I’m reaching out from Sociapi Society, a student-led AI and technology community. We have a campus community at [institution, if confirmed] and publish our current chapter information here: [relevant chapter URL].

If your student opportunities or societies page would help students find this community, would you consider listing the chapter with a link to that page? Please use any description that fits your editorial guidelines. We can share verified chapter details or correct anything that changes.

Thank you,
[Name and role]

## Event host, organizer, or collaborating society

**Subject:** Event recap link for [event name]

Hello [Name],

Thank you for collaborating on [event name]. Sociapi has published a recap with the event purpose, speakers, and approved photographs: [event recap URL].

If you maintain an event archive or recap, would a link to this page be useful to attendees who want the details and photos in one place? We’re happy to link back to your official event page where relevant.

Best,
[Name and role]

## Speaker or mentor

**Subject:** Shareable recap of your Sociapi session

Hello [Name],

Thank you for speaking at [event name]. The recap is available here: [event recap URL]. It includes [verified session detail] and approved event images.

If you share a speaking portfolio, events page, or community update, feel free to reference the recap if it is useful to your audience. Please let us know if your title or organization needs a correction.

Best,
[Name and role]

## Educational publication or technology community

**Subject:** Student-focused resource on [specific topic]

Hello [Name],

I read your [specific article/resource] and thought readers interested in [specific audience need] may also find Sociapi Society’s [specific original report or resource] useful: [destination URL]. It covers [brief, accurate description of original material].

If it fits your editorial standards, would you consider including it as an additional resource? No worries if it does not fit. I’d also welcome corrections or suggestions.

Regards,
[Name and role]

## Suggested sequence

1. Verify the organization, public contact route, and any claimed relationship.
2. Identify a page on that organization's site where the link would help its audience.
3. Personalize the note with a specific reason, the best Sociapi destination, and an easy next action.
4. Send one polite follow-up after 7–10 days; stop if there is no reply or they decline.
5. Record the published URL and classify it accurately as editorial, sponsored, or user-generated. Paid placements should use `rel="sponsored"`; community submissions may use `rel="ugc"` or `nofollow`.
